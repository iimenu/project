Primary menu repository.

For further information, consult profile, https://github.com/iimenu

Find a bug in the menu? Create an [issue](https://github.com/iimenu/project/issues)! We'd be glad to fix it. Make sure to use the issue formatting to as much as your ability as possible, fill in blanks, and keep titles short.
Wish to contribute to the menu, or maybe even fix an issue yourself? Make a [pull request](https://github.com/iimenu/project/issues). We accept almost anything **as long as it works**. Provide your Discord username, and I'll even give you a special role in the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/iidk)!
